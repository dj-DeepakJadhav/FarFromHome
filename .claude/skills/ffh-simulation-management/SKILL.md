# FFH Simulation & Management Rules

**STATUS: ACTIVE & NON-NEGOTIABLE**

The core design challenge of this game is: **Repeating invest-harvest-upgrade loops with visible growth.** 

When modifying or generating any mechanic, feature, or economic balance, you MUST follow these strict rules:

1. **Invest-Harvest-Upgrade Loop**
   - The game MUST maintain a repeating invest, harvest, upgrade loop where output (earnings) reinvests into progression.

2. **Working Economy (The Engine)**
   - What the player earns MUST meaningfully feed back into growth. 
   - The economy is the engine. If your design or code cannot explain where resources come from, where they go, and what makes the player want more, the loop will not hold and is REJECTED.

3. **Visible Progress**
   - There MUST be multiple cycles with visible progress. The player should be able to see their world growing over time.
   - Ensure you can clearly define what the player's world looks like after 15 minutes compared to minute 1 (e.g., new furniture, better tools, rarer items, bigger plots, upgraded room).

4. **Cozy and Satisfying**
   - The game does not need to be stressful to be compelling. Satisfying loops and aesthetic coherence go a long way. Embrace the "cozy" simulation aspect in the room progression.
