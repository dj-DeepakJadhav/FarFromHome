# Gameplay Video Script & Storyboard (2–3 Minutes)

> **Format**: Vertical Portrait capture (390×844) centered on high-contrast background.
> **Duration**: 2:15 – 2:45
> **Thesis to sell**: British deadpan comedy colliding with German municipal precision.
> **Key Goal**: Land the gender-filing gag, then show it powering a real courier-management loop — €20 ➔ €250, the 4-document bureaucracy gauntlet, and 100% offline single-file compliance.

> **Capture rules (read before shooting)**
> - Narration is **post-recorded voice-over by the submitter**. The game itself ships **no recorded audio** — capture with game sound on for SFX and talk-blips, but never imply the game speaks.
> - Never say "learn", "teach", "vocabulary", "pronunciation" or "language game". This is a comedy sim.
> - Every beat below is shootable from the current build with no new work.

---

## 🎬 Beat-by-Beat Timeline

### Beat 1: €20, 28 Days, and a Filing System (0:00 – 0:25)
- **Visual**: Cold open on the 3D Altstadt isometric view outside the station. HUD front and centre: `[€20 / €250] [Day 1 of 28] [📜 📄 📑 💳]`.
- **Narration**: *"You arrive in Lübeck on a 28-day visa with twenty euros and a bicycle. Between you and a residence permit stand four stamped documents, one semester bill, and a country that has strong feelings about doing things properly. You take a courier job. It does not go the way you expect."*
- **Action**: Ride to Kruma Dispatch ➔ meet Nina Voss ➔ clock in for Shift 1.
- **Callout Banner**: `€20 ➔ €250 · 28 DAYS · 4 DOCUMENTS`

### Beat 2: The Joke — An Apple Is A Boy (0:25 – 1:00)
- **Visual**: Push in on the three-tier warehouse shelf. Hold on Klaus, entirely straight-faced.
- **On-screen line (from build)**: Klaus: *"An apple is a boy, a banana is a girl. Pick them right."*
- **Narration**: *"German nouns have a gender. Nobody can tell you why. So of course the warehouse is filed by it. Blue triangle on the bottom, pink circle in the middle, purple square on top. You do not need a word of German — everything is labelled in English. You just have to accept that the apple is a boy."*
- **Action**: Shift 1 picks, icon and rail arriving together. Milk to the pink middle tier, apple to the blue bottom tier, bread to the purple top tier.
- **Callout Banner**: `▲ der · ● die · ■ das — READ BY COLOUR AND SHAPE`

### Beat 3: The Pulse and the Early Pick (1:00 – 1:25)
- **Visual**: Shift 2. The **gender rail pulses** a beat before the item icon resolves — hold the shot so the pulse is unmistakable on camera.
- **Narration**: *"By your second shift the rail lights up before you know what you are picking. Commit early, on nothing but the colour, and you get double. You are now sorting groceries by grammatical gender at speed, and enjoying it, which is the exact moment the joke becomes a game."*
- **Action**: One clean early tap ➔ golden burst ➔ `2.0×` Early Pick flash ➔ streak multiplier climbs.
- **Callout Banner**: `RAIL PULSES FIRST · TAP EARLY · 2.0× BONUS`

### Beat 4: The Ride and the Doorstep (1:25 – 1:50)
- **Visual**: Courier cycles the cobbled Altstadt toward a glowing destination pin, freshness bar ticking down.
- **Narration**: *"Then you ride. Freshness drains, cobblestones do not care, and at the far end is a door with a very specific person behind it — who will absolutely notice whether you said Sie or Du."*
- **Action**: Arrive at Oma Martha's bakery doorway ➔ pick the formal greeting ➔ she beams ➔ generous tip lands on screen.
- **Callout Banner**: `Sie vs. Du · ETIQUETTE IS A TIP MULTIPLIER`

### Beat 5: Invest, Harvest, Upgrade (1:50 – 2:15)
- **Visual**: End-of-shift receipt prints — base pay, streak multiplier, tips, penalties, net.
- **Narration**: *"Every shift closes with a receipt, and every euro on it buys gear that changes the next one. Five upgrades, all of them felt immediately."*
- **Action**: Buy the **E-Bike** (€45) and **Thermal Bag** (€50). Cut straight to the next ride so the speed jump is visible on camera. Flash the catalogue: E-Bike, Thermal Bag, Shelf Labels, Pocket Notepad, Shift Rota Cards.
- **Callout Banner**: `INVEST ➔ HARVEST ➔ UPGRADE ➔ OBSERVE GROWTH`

### Beat 6: Stamped (2:15 – 2:40)
- **Visual**: Rapid-fire montage — €250 paid to Registrar Rita, lease signed with Hans Lokker, Rathaus stamp from Herr Vogel, Sperrkonto unlocked by Frau Weber. Each document icon in the HUD lights as it lands.
- **Beat**: Final scene at the Ausländerbehörde. Dr. Lindemann inspects the dossier for an uncomfortably long moment, then stamps the residence permit.
- **Proof shot**: DevTools in Offline mode, zero network requests, single self-contained `index.html` running at 60 FPS.
- **Narration**: *"Four documents. Twenty-eight days. One very orderly warehouse. Far From Home: Kruma Express — a courier-management sim about being politely, thoroughly outmatched by a filing system."*
- **End Card**: `FAR FROM HOME: KRUMA EXPRESS · Single-file · 100% offline · Portrait 390×844`
