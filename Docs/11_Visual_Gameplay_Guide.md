# Far From Home: Kruma Express — Visual Gameplay & Concept Guide

> **Visual Direction**: Cozy European Graphic Novel / Line-Art Cel Shading (inspired by *Unpacking*, *Wilmot's Warehouse*, and *Papers, Please*).

---

## 1. Student Room Hub (Metagame & Progress Bar)

The student room is your **living progress bar**. You start with a bare mattress on the floor, a single hanging lightbulb, and an orange courier bag. Every shift's earnings allow you to buy room upgrades (wooden bed, study desk, warm lamp, cozy rug, and a pet cat) on your way to funding your 250€ semester fee.

## 2. Warehouse Grocery Picking (The Fulfillment Engine)

Fulfill orders under time pressure with tactile touch interactions.
- **Top Notepad Checklist**: Hand-drawn checklist with live fraction counts and automatic strikethroughs as you pick.
- **Bottom Dock Bar**: Quick-glance dock showing remaining grocery counts.
- **Juicy Feedback**: Items lift with a parabolic arc, squash-and-stretch into your brown paper delivery bag, and explode in golden star sparkles.

## 3. Street Traversal & Tactical Route Selection

Navigate through Berlin's streets with meaningful cargo risk decisions:
- **Kurzer Weg (Cobblestones)**: Fast shortcut that preserves freshness, but threatens fragile items with road bumps and screen shake.
- **Fahrradweg (Bike Lane)**: Smooth paved bike lane that guarantees zero damage to cargo, but takes slightly longer.

## 4. Doorstep Intercom & Itemized Debriefing Receipt

Deliver to historic Berlin Altbau apartments and get itemized economic feedback:
- **Vintage Brass Intercom**: Spring-loaded buzzer panel where you match the customer's floor code (`3. OG`, `HH`, `EG`).
- **Itemized Settlement Receipt**: Full breakdown showing Base Wage, Accuracy Bonus, Streak Multiplier, Freshness Tips, and Cargo Damage Deductions before depositing your net earnings.
